# SurvAIPro: High-Conversion Proposal & Proposal-to-Cash Roadmap

Conan's product spec (July 2026). The proposal must SELL the solution, walking the
customer through a salesperson's decision journey — not just display a quote.

## High-conversion proposal structure (IMPLEMENTED as Authority template)
1. Cover — branding, title, client, address, date, reference
2. Our Understanding of Your Requirements — exec summary: "they understand our problem"
3. Survey Findings — photos, observations, areas needing attention
4. Why This Matters — consequences if untreated (safety, deterioration, cost,
   compliance, appearance). Urgency WITHOUT fear-marketing.
5. Our Recommended Solution — method, equipment, chemicals, access, environmental,
   quality control
6. Why Choose Us — from org profile (years, reviews, insurance, accreditations, USPs)
7. Similar Projects — gallery before/afters + videos, auto-matched by relevance
8. Scope of Works — included / excluded / assumptions / optional extras / site
   responsibilities (anti scope-creep)
9. Programme & Methodology — stages, access, duration, completion process
10. Investment — pricing table, optional extras, VAT, validity, payment terms
11. Supporting Documents — from a Document Library (insurance, accreditations,
    RAMS, T&Cs) with AI recommending per-project docs [NOT YET BUILT]
12. Acceptance & Next Steps — signature, then payment [NOT YET BUILT — see below]

## Proposal-to-Cash workflow (DEPLOYMENT-PHASE — needs Stripe account + hosting)
- Digital acceptance: open → read → sign → (deposit screen if required) → paid →
  Accepted → confirmations both sides
- Deposit rules configured in onboarding/settings, SEPARATE per customer type:
  Residential / Commercial / Property Managers / FM / Housing Assoc / Local
  Authority / Developers. Percentage, fixed, or never. AI detects customer type
  and applies the right rule; auto-calculates deposit + VAT + balance.
- Payments: Stripe (card, Apple Pay, Google Pay), Bank Transfer, UK Open Banking
  "Pay by Bank (recommended)" to cut fees. Klarna future. Business chooses
  offered methods in onboarding. Payment only unlocks AFTER signature.
- Status flow: Draft → Sent → Viewed → Signed → Deposit Paid → Job Won →
  Job Created → Invoice Raised → Final Payment Received
- Admin settings: deposit rules, payment methods, Stripe connection, bank
  details, deposit/invoice wording, proposal validity period.

## Long-term vision
Survey → AI Proposal → Pricing → Supporting Docs → Signature → Deposit → Job →
Invoice → Profit Analysis. Remove every manual step between surveying and getting
paid.
